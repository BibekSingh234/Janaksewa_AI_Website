About Janakpurdham

A City of Heritage, Culture & Community

Janakpurdham is one of Nepal's important cultural and religious destinations. The city is widely known for its temples, sacred ponds, Mithila culture, traditional art and historical connection with the Ram-Janaki tradition.

With the growth of digital technology, citizens increasingly need faster and easier access to public information and services. Janaksewa AI is designed as a digital citizen-assistance platform to bring useful information together in one place.
