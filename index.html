Nepal Land Flood Update

Local Land Administration (Administrative Updates) Under the Land Revenue Act Amendment, local governments (Palikas) handle primary land administrative functions such as ownership transfers (Jagga Pass), property freezes (Rokka), and releases (Phukuwa). This decentralizes operations directly to local municipal Land Management Branches (Bhumi Vyavasthapan Shakha) rather than routing everything through district offices.
