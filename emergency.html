During an Emergency

  • Stay calm and move to a safe location.
  • Contact the appropriate emergency authority.
  • Clearly provide your location and situation.
  • Follow instructions from emergency responders.
  • Help vulnerable people only when it is safe to do so.

Important

  • Do not make false or prank emergency calls.
  • Keep your phone available for emergency communication.
  • Verify contact numbers before using them.
  • For serious danger, prioritize official emergency services.
  • This website should not replace professional emergency response.
